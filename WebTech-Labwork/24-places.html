Popular Places in Nepal

Pokhara Fewa Lake

Fewa Lake is a beautiful freshwater lake located in Pokhara. It is famous for boating and the reflection of the Annapurna mountain range on its calm water. The lake offers a peaceful environment and is a major tourist attraction in Nepal.

Machhapuchre Peak

Machhapuchre, also known as Fishtail Mountain, is one of the most beautiful mountains in Nepal. It is located in the Annapurna region and is considered sacred. The mountain is famous for its unique fishtail shape and stunning natural beauty.

Pashupatinath Temple

Pashupatinath is a sacred Hindu temple located in Kathmandu. It is dedicated to Lord Shiva and is one of the most important religious sites in Nepal. The temple lies on the banks of the Bagmati River and attracts thousands of pilgrims every year.
