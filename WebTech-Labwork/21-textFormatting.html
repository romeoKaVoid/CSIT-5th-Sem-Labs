Different Text Formatting Tags in HTML

1. <strong> - Strong Text

Makes text bold and indicates strong importance

2. <em> - Strong Text

Displays text in italic and gives emphasis.

3. <u> - Strong Text

Underlines the text.

4. <s> - Strong Text

Shows text with a strikethrough (crossed out).

5. <sub> - Strong Text

Displays subscript text (below normal line)

6. <sup> - Strong Text

Displays superscript text (above normal line).

7. <mark> - Strong Text

Highlights text with a background color.

8. <abbr> - Strong Text

Defines an abbreviation (shows full form on hover using title attribute). Like: HTML

9. <del> - Strong Text

Shows deleted text with a strikethrough.

10. <ins> - Strong Text

Shows inserted text, usually underlined.
