History of ASCOL

Amrit Science Campus (ASCOL) formerly known as Public Science College (PUSCOL) is a government institution, with partial decentralization, affiliated to Tribhuvan University.The campus was established in 2013 B.S. With the view to promote the study of science and technology in Nepal by the founder Principal of Public Science College, Late Amrit Prasad Pradhan, Professor of Chemistry.
